## Judging priorities

1. A reliable, judge-readable path: filed reason → two evidence tracks → exact source → unresolved question → useful next action.
2. Honest provenance: seeded demo, recorded AI replay, and real PayPal sandbox actions visibly distinct.
3. Product safety and usefulness: private cases, scoped evidence, rate limits, quote-check boundaries, and review before any PayPal action.
4. Submission polish: one sub-three-minute video, current Devpost copy, an accessible live site and public repo.

Adding an unrelated sponsor integration would make the product harder to understand and test. The strongest use of existing sponsor tools is to make AG Grid/Studio and Bryntum support the same case evidence journey.
